# Invoices (list, detail, form)

Scope: the Phase 1 screens. Mode: Operate. Audience: a freelancer at a laptop, checking what is unpaid and adding an invoice in under a minute. Chosen direction: Gardens Guide Map, picked by the user on the decision page over the assigned Timetable. Unresolved: dashboard and settings screens arrive in later phases and inherit this world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Unpaid money is territory on a guide map: one flat green lobe holds the totals and the list reads below it. Refuses the white dashboard of grey rail, three metric cards, and status pills.

OWN-WORLD: Cream water #F5F1E4, land green #8ED462, ink #2C2E2A, flat with no gradients or shadows. Controls are capsules in yellow #F5E211, coral #FF705D, blue #2BA0FF, lilac #EBC1FF, each with a round ink chip on its trailing edge. One grotesk (Bricolage Grotesque) at one weight; hierarchy by size only; display at 0.95 leading, -0.04em.

STORY: The freelancer sees how much is unpaid and how much is late, scans late invoices first, then adds or opens one.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Standard top bar (brand, Invoices, yellow New invoice capsule). A green headland fills the top left, its coast leaving the frame at the top and left. On it, the page heading at display size and one sentence giving unpaid and overdue totals. Below: Unpaid, Paid, Archived capsule tabs, then the Overdue table (coral label) and the Due table on white sheets. Signature move: the lobe; signature interaction: the chip slides with a 0.4s overshoot on hover.

FORM: Gardens Guide Map, a dealt challenger the user adopted (catalog aww2-zoo-gardens-guide-map); seed:79197cb7a506627b.
